## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one emotional logo or seal, not an illustration scene. Keep a single symbolic center. Flowers, branches, a halo or a natural pattern may enclose the subject as protection, company, growth or memory, and must not become a decoration pile. Place the small mark centered, off-center or slightly floating in extreme white space. Reject a filled vignette, unrelated ornaments and a loss of the core identity.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
